How It Works

🔍

1 — Discover Events

Go to Events and browse upcoming university, departmental, and organizational events. Click Read More on any event for full details.

✅

2 — Register Instantly

On the event detail page, click Register for this Event. You'll need an account — sign up here if you don't have one yet.

📲

3 — Attend with QR

After registering, open My Events to find your personal QR code. Present it at the event venue — staff will scan it to record your attendance.
